canvas: express zoom limits in percent and add zoom presets

The toolbar shows zoom as a percentage, so the zoom limits are now defined in the same unit (`MIN_SCALE = 10`, `MAX_SCALE = 800`).

Also adds:
- `clampZoom(pct)` to keep a percentage inside that range
- `ZOOM_PRESETS` and `nextZoomPreset(pct, dir)` for stepping between common zoom levels
