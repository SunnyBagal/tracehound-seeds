ws: namespace per-socket replies as op:ack / op:error

Acks and errors are only ever sent to the socket that submitted the op, so they now go through a small `reply()` helper and use namespaced types that can't be mistaken for room broadcasts:

- `op_ack` → `op:ack`
- `error` → `op:error`, which now also includes `roomId` and `shapeId` so the client can tell which operation failed

Room broadcasts (`op`, `chat`) keep their existing types.
